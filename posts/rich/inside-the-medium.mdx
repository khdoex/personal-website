this site can do more than text now. posts can carry figures that build
themselves while you read, small experiments you can poke at, and notes in
the margin for the parenthetical thoughts.<MarginNote>like this one. it
stays out of the way until you want it.</MarginNote>

the point is not decoration. interp work is spatial: directions, distances,
geometry. describing a direction in words is like describing a song by
listing its notes. you can do it, but why would you.
